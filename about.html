Personal Background

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering Technology at NJIT (expected May 2028). My interest in engineering started from a curiosity about how things work, especially electronic devices and embedded systems. I've been learning through hands-on projects and lab research that combine circuit design, programming, and physical fabrication.

At PiD Labs I worked as an undergraduate research assistant on a jackhammer telerehabilitation simulator, then as lead researcher on a HAVS grip-tracking drill. Through coursework and these projects I've gained experience with embedded firmware, schematic capture, EMI-aware analog sensing, and rapid prototyping in CAD, 3D printing, and machining. I enjoy taking ideas from concept to a physical prototype that can actually be tested.
