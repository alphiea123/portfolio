Personal Background

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering Technology at NJIT. My interest in engineering started from a curiosity about how things work, especially electronic devices and embedded systems. I've been learning through hands-on projects that combine circuit design, programming, and physical fabrication.

Through my coursework and personal projects, I've gained experience working with embedded systems, PCB assembly, and rapid prototyping using CAD software, 3D printing, and laser cutting. I enjoy taking ideas from concept to physical reality, whether that's designing a custom PCB or creating a modular system that can be 3D printed. The combination of hardware and software challenges keeps me engaged and constantly learning.
